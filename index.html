European health data, one question at a time.

healthviz is a playground for exploring and presenting open health data from Europe. Each entry takes public data, mostly from Eurostat, and turns one question into an interactive visualisation.

About

Every entry is a snapshot. Its data stays exactly as it was retrieved until the entry is updated on purpose, and each entry shows when its data was retrieved and when the source last changed it.

All data comes from open sources and is credited on each entry with its licence. The visualisations describe populations, not individuals, and are not medical advice.

healthviz is a personal side project, built in spare time to try out ways of showing data clearly.
