Where in Europe you live longest

In 2023, a baby born in Comunidad de Madrid (Spain) could expect to live 86.1 years, 12.2 years longer than one born in Severozapaden (Bulgaria). That is the spread across 242 regions in the EU-27. In Germany it ranges from 79.2 in Sachsen-Anhalt to 82.8 in Tübingen.

Notes

Life expectancy at birth is the average number of years a newborn would live if current death rates by age stayed the same. It is a summary of mortality in that year, not a forecast. Regions follow NUTS 2024. Where Eurostat publishes older years only for former regions with different borders (Portugal, the Netherlands, Norway, Croatia), those years are shown as no data (hatched) rather than redrawn. The colour scale is set per sex and stays fixed across years. The dip in 2020–2022 reflects COVID-19; Turkey's 2023 low reflects the February earthquakes. Outermost regions are shown as insets below the map.
